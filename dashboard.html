Illinois Media Deep Dive

What Illinois news is focused on — discovered from coverage, not predefined. Counts, not sentiment.
How to read this. This tracks Illinois civic coverage — government, policy, elections, courts, schools, public safety, housing, budget — with sports, entertainment, and lifestyle filtered out (the archive keeps everything; civic is a reversible lens). Themes are emergent (surfaced from the articles, then normalized so trends stay comparable); entities are raw mentions. “Surging” means a theme is above its own recent normal, not just high-volume. Sources are a curated set of Illinois outlets (see “Source composition”), not the whole state press; downstate is thinner because legacy dailies are paywalled. No sentiment or tone is inferred.
